Read it by the ink

Every figure in the ledger is written in the ink of its source, so you can tell a reported fact from a model cue or your own assumption at a glance.

  • Read 10-KGold tick: matched to a filing

    A reported SEC fact for that period. The link opens the filing document; the XBRL tag names the concept.

  • Percentile / 100Dashed box: AI triage

    An Isolation Forest percentile against earlier company-years. It points to where to read first. It is not a verdict, a default probability or advice.

  • −10%Lilac pencil: your assumption

    Scenario inputs you type. They are calculated in your browser and kept apart from the reported facts.

  • Profit above cashRed ink: worth a question

    Profit running ahead of cash, an overdue snapshot or a failed load. A prompt to read further, never a conclusion.
